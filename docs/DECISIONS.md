# Implementation decisions

One line per non-obvious decision: what was chosen, why, what was tested. Design-level decisions live in DESIGN.md; this file records the implementer's deviations and findings.

- 2026-10-02 · Product name "Smidge" is the architect's proposal, not yet approved by Hunter. Code names use `cia`, the brand lives only in `packages/brand/brand.json`, so a rename touches one file plus repo names.
- 2026-10-02 · wasi-sdk 25 is expected at `/opt/wasi-sdk` (symlink on the dev VM). The spike that proved mozjpeg/libwebp/libdeflate/zstd compile for wasm32 with that sysroot plus `-D__wasi__` is recorded in `.cargo/config.toml`.
- 2026-10-02 · Toolchain pinned to 1.98.1 (the version on the dev VM and both lab VMs). Hunter's Windows PC has 1.92; rustup installs the pin on first build there.
- 2026-10-02 · Spike gate (DESIGN 2.5 item 4) passed on wasm32-unknown-unknown: `cia-wasm-spike` (cia-mozjpeg over mozjpeg-sys 2.2.3, cia-webp over libwebp-sys 0.14.4, wasm-bindgen 0.2.100) encodes a 640x480 JPEG and lossless/lossy WebP under Node 22; PIL decodes all three at the right size and the lossless WebP is pixel-exact. libc symbols the link needed from `cia-wasm-libc`: malloc, calloc, realloc, free, qsort (libwebp palette/Huffman sort), strlen, abort, exit, getenv, fprintf, fflush, fputs, fputc, fwrite, snprintf, stderr/stdout (mozjpeg jerror/jdatadst); memcpy/memset/memmove/memcmp were NOT needed (compiler-builtins provides them). libwebp-sys ≥0.14 defines WEBP_USE_THREAD under its `std` feature, which needs pthreads, so cia-webp enables `std` for non-wasm targets only. Module size: 715 KB from cargo, 691 KB after wasm-bindgen, 670 KB after `wasm-opt -O3`, 174 KB gzip -9. Debian's wasm-opt 108 additionally needs `--enable-sign-ext` (Rust 1.98 emits sign-extension ops by default); add it to the xtask flag list.
- 2026-10-02 · `cia-video-plan` aims at `Budget.raw_budget_bytes` (cia-core's `ResolvedLimit.budget_for(Video)`, limit minus `safety_bytes`, Discord Free 20,905,984). DESIGN 3.5.4 calls that number `hard_bytes` and subtracts one more byte (20,905,983); cia-core does not, so the planner follows cia-core. The worked example therefore gives total 2,191,133 bps instead of the design's 2,191,134 and a prediction of 19,238,810 bytes instead of 19,238,816; still "about 19.2 MB", still 720p30 Good.
- 2026-10-02 · Video overhead (3.5.2) is evaluated per rung with that rung's frame rate, since the formula needs an fps before a rung is chosen. The design's example uses 30 fps throughout, which is the chosen rung; the 60 fps start rung now pays for its own frames (95,796 bytes at 70 s instead of 66,396). Fractional frame rates round the per-second term up.
- 2026-10-02 · Refusal max-duration (3.5.5): the design's `min_bps = floor video + lowest audio rung` is unreachable under the 3.5.3 rule, which picks a higher audio rung once 15 percent of the total covers it (at the 360p floor the total is 547,840 bps and the rule picks 64 kb/s, not 32). The planner solves the bound against the audio rule that actually runs, iterates twice on the overhead as designed, then checks whole seconds against the real planner. Jayden's clip on Discord Free with NVENC gets max 4 min 37 s (277 s), not the design's 4 min 54 s; 294 s refuses because 64 kb/s audio leaves 452,707 bps for video, under the 483,840 floor. Tested: exactly max_duration_ms plans at 360p30, one second more refuses, for both margins.
- 2026-10-02 · `max_short_edge` (3.5.4 "drop rungs above it") is applied by scaling the start rung down to the cap at the start frame rate, not by deleting rungs. Deleting would lose 60 fps for every 1440p60 or 4K60 source on Discord (max 1080), since the 60 fps rung exists only at the start size. A 1440p60 clip for Discord now has 1080p60 first, then 1080p30, 720p30... WhatsApp (720, max_fps 30) gives 720p30, 540p30, 480p30, 360p30 as the design says.
- 2026-10-02 · Even dimensions follow FFmpeg's `scale=-2:H` exactly: `av_rescale(h, in_w, in_h * 2) * 2`, the nearest multiple of two from the real source aspect (854x480 -> 640x360, 2560x1080 -> 1706x720, 1081x1921 -> 480x852). The source size itself rounds down to even so no rung is larger than the source.
- 2026-10-02 · `keep_original_or_remux` always encodes a source with more than one audio track, even when it fits and its codecs are allowed: a copy or remux keeps both tracks and chat players play only the first, so the mix the user story expects ("both tracks audible") needs an encode.
- 2026-10-02 · `retry_scale` clamps the 3.5.8 factor to at most 1 and keeps the audio rung; the caller accepts an undershoot (below 85 percent of the budget) as is and never calls it. Moving "down one rung" continues down until a rung's floor is met, None when none remains.
- 2026-10-02 · The task brief quoted Jayden's clip as "2 min 4 s -> 720p, about 19.6 MB, Good"; DESIGN 1.3 and 3.5.4 say 1 min 10 s and 19.2 MB, and the 3.5.4 formula at 124 s gives 540p30, 19.2 MB, Okay (video 1,106,077 bps is 0.040 bpp at 720p, under the 0.050 floor). The 70 s design example is the required unit test; the 124 s case is asserted at what the formula produces.
